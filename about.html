About this page

This page is the result of a published paper authored by Albert Busca and Albert Carreras. This webpage shows some of the main insights and results from the paper. It displays some diverse interactive tools that mainly use the EU-SPI data published by the European Commission. You can see the link to the full paper below.

A core-periphery comeback? Clustering European Union Social Progress Index (EU-SPI). Busca & Carreras, 2025. Journal of Regional Research.

About the data

This page offers interactive tools reproducing most of the data and figures that can be seen in the paper. However, reading the paper is still recommended to fully understand the results shown here.

We believe the EU-SPI is an incredibly powerful tool for policy-makers and researchers. To make working with the index as straightforward as possible, a replication package for R (euspir) is available on Github, which allows you to download the official EU-SPI data published by the European Commission and experiment with the underlying methodology.

The official EU-SPI data and documentation from the European Commission can be accessed on their website here.
